## 📌 Project Overview

The **YouTube Analysis Report** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate **YouTube channel performance, video metadata, and engagement behavior** at scale. The report analyzes billions of views to uncover how **content quality (HD vs SD), captions, publishing trends, video duration, and engagement metrics** influence overall performance.

This project demonstrates advanced **data modeling, DAX-based KPIs, and visual storytelling**, making it ideal for data analytics and business intelligence portfolios.

## 📈 Visualizations Breakdown

### 🎥 Total Titles by Definition (HD vs SD)

* Donut chart shows **99.63% of content is published in HD**, highlighting industry-wide adoption of high-quality video formats.
* SD content accounts for a negligible share, indicating minimal impact on modern engagement trends.

### 📆 Total Titles by Year

* Area chart reveals **steady growth in content production**, followed by a **sharp spike around 2022–2023**.
* This surge reflects the rapid expansion of creators, platforms, and short- and long-form video strategies.

### 📝 Total Titles by Caption

* **21.42%** of videos include captions (*True*), while **78.58%** do not (*False*).
* This insight highlights a major opportunity for creators to improve **accessibility, SEO, and audience retention**.

### 📺 Channels – Views per Day

* Bar chart highlights top-performing channels by daily views:

  * **THEBLACK…** – 5.3M views/day
  * **SMTOWN** – 2.9M views/day
  * Other channels show a steep drop-off, illustrating a **power-law distribution** in audience reach.

### ⏱️ Duration vs Engagement Rate

* Scatter plot analyzes the relationship between **video length and engagement**.
* Engagement is highly concentrated in **short-to-medium duration videos**, with diminishing returns for longer content.

### 🔥 Titles – Views per Day

* Bar chart identifies **top-performing video titles** based on daily view count.
* Demonstrates how **content topic, timing, and audience interest** drive viral performance.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Source:** YouTube Kaggle Dataset (or equivalent YouTube metadata source)
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and feature preparation
* **DAX:**

  * Engagement Rate calculation
  * Like & Comment Ratios
  * Viral Score metric
* **Custom Visuals:** Donut charts, scatter plots, and advanced bar charts
* **Interactive Features:** Cross-filtering, slicers, and drill-through analysis

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* HD content dominates YouTube and is now the **industry standard**.
* Content creation has **accelerated rapidly since 2022**, intensifying competition.
* Captions remain underutilized despite their accessibility and engagement benefits.
* A small number of channels capture a **disproportionate share of daily views**.
* Optimal engagement is achieved within **short-to-medium video durations**.
